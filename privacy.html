WXCON — PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: February 2026

DATA COLLECTION

WXCON does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app does not require an account, registration, or sign-in of any kind.

LOCATION DATA

WXCON requests your location solely to provide a local hourly weather forecast. Your coordinates are sent directly from your device to the Open-Meteo API to retrieve forecast data. Your location is never stored, logged, or transmitted to any server operated by us. If you decline location access, the app defaults to Dublin and all other features continue to work normally.

NETWORK REQUESTS

WXCON connects to two external services:

1. Met Éireann — to retrieve current weather warning data for Ireland.

2. Open-Meteo — to retrieve hourly forecast data based on your location.

These requests are made directly from your device. No data passes through any intermediary server operated by us.

LOCAL DATA STORAGE

WXCON stores your selected colour theme preference locally on your device using standard app settings. No other data is persisted between sessions.

ANALYTICS AND TRACKING

WXCON does not include any analytics frameworks, crash reporters, advertising SDKs, or third-party tracking of any kind.

CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

WXCON does not collect data from anyone, including children.

CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a revised date.

CONTACT

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue on the WXCON GitHub repository.
